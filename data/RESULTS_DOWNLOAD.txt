The released run data (data/results/: 537 result pickles and the shuffle-order
files) is distributed as a single archive:

  Download link: https://huggingface.co/datasets/subtask-skill-transfer/subtask-skill-transfer-data/resolve/main/subtask_skill_transfer_results.tar.gz

  SHA256: b729a536fea812ae7f2596e9b5e179790a4f8fa80cf675d3e258cb1af2fb2ac5

Setup after download:

  cd data/
  tar xzf subtask_skill_transfer_results.tar.gz

This creates data/results/. The paper figures notebook (AAA_final_paper_plot.ipynb)
reads from that directory directly. The trajectory viewer (inspector.html) needs its
per-task HTML fragments, which are not included in the archive; rebuild them once
from the pickles (standard library only, no extra dependencies):

  python scripts/build_inspector.py
